## Overview

This audit maps recurring tasks from my week (studies, FlyRank internship, freelance design work, and personal projects) and classifies each based on where AI genuinely helps, where it should only assist with review, and where it should stay out entirely. This is the foundation for the AI Fluency track — the classifications and target tasks here carry forward into FL-02 through FL-04.

## 4. Target Tasks for FL-02 → FL-04

Three recurring tasks I'll reuse across the next assignments, with measurable definitions of "done well":

### 1. Debugging git/.gitignore or history issues
**Classification:** Delegate to AI with review
**Done well means:** AI identifies the root cause (e.g. which file/encoding is causing the leak) on the first or second attempt, the fix doesn't require me to manually redo git history more than once, and no API keys or secrets end up in a subsequent commit.

### 2. Writing FlyRank weekly assignment reports/summaries
**Classification:** Delegate to AI with review
**Done well means:** First draft captures the technical details accurately with zero factual errors about what I actually built, requires only light editing for voice (not a rewrite), and is ready to submit in under 15 minutes of my review time.

### 3. Tailoring CVs/cover letters for internship applications
**Classification:** Collaborate with AI
**Done well means:** The final version highlights the specific project/skill match for that role (e.g. MedicAsk for a GenAI role) without inventing or exaggerating experience, reads in my voice rather than generic corporate language, and I can produce a tailored version for a new role in under 30 minutes.
